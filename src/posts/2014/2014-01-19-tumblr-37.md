Team members Michaela and Christine spent the weekend at the NC Seaperch training learning how to build the underwater ROV.  The training was spent soldering, waterproofing, and assembling until the newly named Seapup was ready to enter the pool for its first swim!

Seaperch is a middle and high school program where teams compete in various underwater competitions after assembling and modifying their robot.  It’s a great way to teach some basic engineering and skills, such as soldering, while having a great amount of fun.

[You can find out more about Seaperch here!](http://www.seaperch.org/)
