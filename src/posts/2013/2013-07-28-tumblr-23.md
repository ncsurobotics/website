07-28-2013

Most of the team was surprised to learn how open and friendly all the competing teams are with each other throughout the competition.  As put by another team: “We aren’t against each other, we’re against the course.”

Throughout everything teams helped each other out by lending a hand or supplies as needed and gave advise to newer teams just starting out.

The team from Maryland was stationed two tents down but were constantly entertaining with their singing and high spirits despite having several problems of their own.  Their acoustics members helped the NCSU team so much and both teams worked to swap ideas and enjoyed just hanging out.  NCSU cheered Maryland on as they grabbed the wild card spot and were able to compete in finals.

Next to the team was Embry-Riddle from Daytona Beach Florida, a new team that originally competed only in Roboboat.  This team also brought along a middle school team of homeschoolers that actually managed to out score them in the semi-finals.  Embry-Riddle turned out to be the team rooming next to NCSU at the hotel as well.  While waiting for the last team meeting the two teams sat down for an intense game of UNO and Spoons which was a lot of fun.

We hope to see both teams back out here again next year.
