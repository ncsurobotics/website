07-27-2013

After another late night and an early day for a lot of the team it was time once again to spend time fixing more problems to prepare for semi-finals.

The team had to give up their practice run due to a critical problem and thought there would be no way to be fixed up before semi-finals.  Luckily Florida University willingly switched time slots with the team so they would not be on deck until 3:00 pm.

Special thanks to FU for helping us out and giving more help later on.  Also thank you to the team from Maryland who took the time to answer any questions the team had.
