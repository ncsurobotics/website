7-30-2013

The team’s last day in San Diego lend to the team finally taking the time to play tourist.  First stop after lunch at In-and-Out burger was the beach where everyone got to wade in the Pacific ocean and some of the guys decided to take a dip.

Afterwards the team explored some small shops and the pier before finding an ice cream shop that made custom cookie ice cream sandwiches.

Next the team wondered downtown stopping in a few stores and checking out Hardrock.  Finally the team took a break at the park enjoying the sun that had not been seen for most of the days here.

Good-bye San Diego.
