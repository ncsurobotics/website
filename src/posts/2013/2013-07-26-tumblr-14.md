07-26-2013 

After a lot of problems including the team’s internet router dying and the thrusters not responding, it was finally decided to take the robot out shopping for parts and advice.

The team luckily found a small electronics store with someone willing to help out as much as possible.  Three hours later the team was back hungry but ready to test.  This time things ran smoothly.
