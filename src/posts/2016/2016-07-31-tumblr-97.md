*July 29th-30th 2016:*

Semi-finals always are the busiest time of the week for the team.  Between preparing for the competition runs and talking to visiting companies and spectators the team had their hands full.

The team was only able to complete the path and gate challenges during their runs due to some software issues, however acoustics is fully functional and the team plans to attempt for a wildcard slot.

The team also had their presentation explaining their team structure and robot to a panel of judges.  All and all it has been a very busy couple of days, but productive and fun.

Good luck to everyone already qualified for finals.  We’ll be posting more pictures soon!
