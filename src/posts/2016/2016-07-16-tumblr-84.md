*Software:*

The Software team has been hard at work learning and updating all of SeaWolf VI’s code in order for the robot to complete more tasks at competition.

The top picture shows the team’s IMU simulation allowing for direction detection to be tested outside the robot by simply turning the robot’s electrical board different angles.

The middle picture demonstrates SeaWolf’s vision processing software as it tries to detect and identify the buoys for the Scuttle Boat task.
