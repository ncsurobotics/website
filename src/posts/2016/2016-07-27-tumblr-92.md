*July 26th 2016:*

The first day of Robosub started early for the team having stayed up late the night before and arriving at Transdec at 6:00am to get their first testing time.

The day went on to be incredibly productive for the team.  SeaWolf qualified during the team’s first run at 8:00am allowing the rest of the day to be spent testing code and gathering footage.

The team will compete in semi-finals Friday and Saturday allowing the rest of the week to be spent practicing and making needed tweaks.
