*Pool Tests:*

It is not unusual for the team to pack up their gear and spend the weekends at the pool on campus testing the robot and any new additions. These tests allow the team a chance to gather footage of the props through the Seawolf VI’s cameras and time to test new software to see how the robot is running.

Pool tests will continue to be held into the summer leading up to the robot’s ship out day July 18th.
