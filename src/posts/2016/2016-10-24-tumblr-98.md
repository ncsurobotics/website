We can not thank our sponsors like [Altium](http://www.altium.com/) enough for all they do to support our team!

Having access to the latest CAD software and tools allows our team members not only to work on the robot’s continued development, but to practice valuable professional skills they would otherwise be unable to learn.

This year the team will take full advantage of the provided software to work on revisions to Seawolf’s current boards along with adding new status LEDs and making changes to connectors on our existing design.

This sponsorship is valued at $43,000, but to our team members who use it daily it’s priceless.

The URC team looks forward to showing off just what they can create with Altium’s software in this upcoming 2017 season!
