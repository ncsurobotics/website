*July 27th 2016:*

After another long day of practice the team has managed to fix most of the problems encountered and been able to continue to develop their mission code.  SeaWolf VI hit the pool in Transdec again gathering footage and testing newly adjust buoy, path, and hedge code.

A few minor roadbumps were hit as the team’s kill switch stopped operating properly, which was quickly fixed thanks to the hard work of the electrical team.

The team plans to test into the night to troubleshoot a camera feed issue that has resulted in a few dropped frames.
