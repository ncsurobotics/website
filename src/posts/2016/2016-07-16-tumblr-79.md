*Outreach:*

This year the team once again spent March and April volunteering at the state’s FIRST Robotics Competitions. This was the first year for NC to host events using the district model meaning there were four district qualifying  competitions all over the state and a final state competition at the end of the season.  The URC team proudly had members volunteering at all competitions in various roles including scorekeeping, robot inspection, and queuing.  The team also aided local teams in preparation for the competition by helping them review designs, mentoring, and coaching. 

FIRST Robotics is a yearly competition that begins in January with a six week build season followed by competitions starting in late February and March.  Teams are comprised of high school and middle school aged students competing on school or community established teams. North Carolina is home to around 50 teams and growing each year.  

This year’s competition was entitled Stronghold and was designed to be like a castle defense game.  In alliances of three, teams had to work together to take down their enemy defenses and fire boulders into the enemy’s tower.  At the end of the match teams could climb the tower (seen above) for extra capture points.
