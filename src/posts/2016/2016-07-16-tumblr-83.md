*Hoggard Trip:* 

This year the team made a special trip two hours away to meet with a high school JROTC, computer science class, and school Seaperch program to give a demonstration of SeaWolf VI and talk about the concept of underwater autonomous vehicles.

Despite the rainy weather, the team was able to spend the day showing the robot off to four individual class groups at the local YWCA and giving a small additional presentation to the school’s robotics team at the school.  Students were able to ask questions and see SeaWolf up close before the team showed off its new torpedo and dropper peripherals in the water.  Students were also able to drive a Seaperch ROV around the pool, competing with each other to pick up dive rings and collect them in a submerged bucket.

We were thrilled to be invited down to present and hope to return in the future along with to be able aid in the area’s growing Seaperch program.
