*Outreach:*

This year team member Michaela took on an additional role during the FIRST Robotics season as the coach for local team, The Gadget Girls (4816). The Gadget Girls is a Girl Scout team and one of two all girls teams in North Carolina.  

The team performed exceptionally well this season competition at two district events being a must pick team capable of taking down all of the field defenses.  The team was popular among judges for their team spirit and simplistic robot design.  They took home the Imagery Award, for attractiveness in engineering and outstanding visual aesthetic integration of machine and team appearance, at both competitions as well as finishing twice in the top five alliances during eliminations. 

The Gadget Girls works to encourage girls to learn technical skills through hands-on engineering activities as well as developing other professional skills including presenting, designing, and building a good teamwork ethic.  The team includes girls in both middle and high school of various skill levels from around the county.  Their robot was completely designed and built by the team members with guidance from their mentors.
