*Last Pool Test:*

The team headed to the pool for one last round of testing before SeaWolf VI is to be packed up and shipped to San Diego for the Robosub competition! This test session yielded great results with the robot successfully completing several tasks  and was able to demonstrate its ability to track the pinger in the water.

We are excited to be heading out to competition in a week.
