*July 28th 2016:*

Another long day of practice at Robosub for SeaWolf and the team.  SeaWolf has developed an issue the team has not seen before with the camera streams that allow the robot to navigate freezing and crashing.  Other small problems have arisen, but the team has been working endlessly to solve them in between practice runs in Transdec.

On a positive note the team took fifth place on their paper out of forty-eight teams allowing them to pick a late run slot for both days of semi-finals.  The URC team is hopeful they will be able to fix all remaining problems in the pool tonight!
