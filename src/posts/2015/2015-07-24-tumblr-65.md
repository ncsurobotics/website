July 23rd:

The last day of practice runs for the team started early at 8:15 am with the team focusing on running mission code and working with the new camera modifications.

A large part of the day was spent testing the waterproofing of the hull after having another mysterious leak.  Team members determined that a loose Fisher connector being pressed on by the inner board may have been the source of the leak.  With the O-ring replaced and the Fisher tightened SeaWolf VI seemed ready to go again.

Time has been well spent talking to other teams about new design ideas and getting to see how everyone has been doing this competition.  The team is happy to have made friends with many of the other teams helping to share parts, tools, and advice so everyone can succeed while in TRANSDEC.

At the team meeting the team decided to do their first semi-final run at 6:30pm tomorrow (July 24th) with an early practice run in the morning.

SeaWolf VI also joined the other teams preparing late at night in the pool at the hotel to test a few more changes made to the board that seemed to have solved many of the issues.

Wish us luck as we begin semi-final runs!
