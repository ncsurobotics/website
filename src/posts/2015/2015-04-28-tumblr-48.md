Vex Tournament February 14th 2015:

Members from the URC team volunteered at the local Raleigh Vex tournament serving as judges and spending the day talking to each team.

Our members had the important job of deciding the middle and high school teams that would receive the Judge’s Award, the Design Award, and the Excellence Award that would qualify the teams for the State competition.
