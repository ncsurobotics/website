July 25th:

Semi-finals have begun. All the teams here at RoboSub have been busy making last minute modifications with the hope to get the highest score possible to move on to finals.

At the start of the day the team had their presentation for the panel of judges as part of static judging.  During this presentation the team discussed SeaWolf VI, how the team operates, and their plans for the future.  URC placed 12 out of 28 teams in this judging field.  These points will count towards their final score.

The team ran into major electrical problems after their first practice run resulting from some corrosion on the board. The rest of the day was spent trouble-shooting all the problems until the team’s semi-final run at the end of the day.

The URC team was not alone struggling with their run.  Many of the scores were unusually low for the year, but everyone has a second chance to compete tomorrow.

Seen in the last picture are the scores after semi-finals.  Each symbol corresponds to a task on the challenge course.  Teams must start by passing through the gate for the run to officially count.
