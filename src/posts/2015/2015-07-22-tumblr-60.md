July 21st:

The team arrived early in the morning to reserve a spot to start gathering footage and working on qualifying for semi-finals.

During the team’s second run of the morning SeaWolf made it successfully through the gate allowing the URC team to qualify for semi-finals, which will take place Friday and Saturday this week.

The rest of the day was spent hopping in and out of the pool to gather footage and test various mission code. Overall it was a very successful day!
