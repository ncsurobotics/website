# North Carolina Regional FRC Competition Recap!

Photos by: Danny Levenson. 

The URC team had a fun, but busy weekend volunteering at the FRC Competition held March 19th-21st.  Team members served as referees, scorekeepers, inspectors, and members of the field reset and queuing teams.  

The competition began Thursday with teams unpacking their robots and participating in practice matches all day.   Friday qualification matches began with teams looking to score as many points as possible to up their rank and show their robot off for potential alliance partners. 

Saturday began with the last few rounds of qualifying matches before it was time for the final alliances to be selected.  This year over 50 teams competed from around the local area and from as far away as Pennsylvania.  However, only the top 8 teams could pick their choice of teammates for the finals. 

Congratulations to the Team 1225, Team 2059, and Team 900 for being the winning alliance of the tournament and moving on the Nationals.
