North Carolina’s FRC Regional Competition!

Photos by: Danny Levenson

The URC team would like to congratulate all the teams that competed in the FRC Regional Competition held March 19th-21st.

URC team members spent the season helping out a few teams from around the local area.  These teams have only a short time to design, build, and prepare for the competition and we were happy to assist in any way we could.

*Teams:*
- Team 4816- The Gadget Girls
- Team 435- Robodogs
- Team 5446- The Pink Detectives
- Team 5511- Cortechs Robotics
- Team 5518- Techno Wolves

The Gadget Girl’s is one of the only local all girl teams made up with Girl Scouts that all share a passion in robotics.

The Pink Detectives team were a rookie team this year and one of the youngest teams comprised mostly of middle school students.  They were selected to be members of the final alliance allowing them to compete in finals. 

Team Cortechs Robotics, also a rookie team, asked the URC team for advice on their robot design early in the season.  Our team was impressed with their professionalism and the team ranked 3rd at the end of qualification allowing them to advance to the finals. 

The Techno Wolves are another rookie team that now meet on NCSU’s campus and reached out to the URC team for advice on their first season. 

URC team member Ryan spent the season working with his old team the RoboDogs helping them to design and building along with serving as a mentor at the competition. 

The Underwater Robotics team would also like to give a shout out to team 900 and team 3737 the former teams of a few of our members.
