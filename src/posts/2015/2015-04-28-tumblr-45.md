The URC team traveled to volunteer with the off season THOR FRC event on November 1st.  Team members served as referees, scorekeepers, and general volunteers throughout the day also making sure to break and have some fun dancing in true FIRST fashion.

The THOR event allows teams to practice in a friendly competition before the kick off of the new build season.  Many teams use this as a chance to test new designs and train new drivers to help them be successful in the up coming year!
