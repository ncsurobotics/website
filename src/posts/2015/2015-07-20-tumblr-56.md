# July 19th 2015- July 20th 2015

The URC team has safely arrived in San Diego for competition and has immediately begun preparing for the kick off of the Robosub competition tomorrow.

The crate arrived this morning allowing the team to unpack and reassemble the robot.  Unfortunately, the team’s hull was vacuumed shut in transit leading to SeaWolf getting to enjoy a nice hot shower in order to heat up the air inside and increase the pressure until the end cap could be safely popped off. 

The team will be spending the rest of the day putting everything back together before hitting the pool for the first time at 10pm tonight!

Competition begins at 7am tomorrow morning.  

[Check out the Robosub Website for more information!](http://www.auvsifoundation.org/foundation/competitions/robosub/)
