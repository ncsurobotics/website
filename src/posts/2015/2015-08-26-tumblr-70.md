July 26th:

Last day of competition!

The morning began with the second chance runs for all the teams that didn’t qualify for Finals.  The URC team had high hopes of scoring the required 2000 point amount needed for consideration after spending the night before discussing a plan of action.

During our final run however, Seawolf VI seemed to be a little confused as it started going in circles.  Unfortunately it seemed to be a software issue we were unable to fix in time for a full run, but this gave the team a chance to talk to visitors at the booth and to spectate the Finalist teams.

The URC team would like to thank everyone who helped us during competition week and to wish good luck to all the teams we made friends with from all over the world.  Robosub is truly a unique competition that allows a great hands on experience.

We would also like to congratulate the winning teams!

See you next year!
