---
title: Multi-Hull Waterproofing
sort: 3
---

Seawolf VIII makes use of three waterproof hulls, A cut-to size main hull with custom tapped endcaps that contains all of Seawolfs electrical systems, and two Blue Robotics battery hulls that provide power to Seawolf VIII's systems.

Seawolf VIII's Multi-Hull system allows for rapid replacement of spent batteries, and provides more room in the main hull for custom electronics systems.
