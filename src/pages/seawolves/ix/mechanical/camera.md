---
title: Custom Camera Hull
sort: 2
---

While previous robots used liquid gasket to seal camera hulls, SeaWolf IX has made the switch to a racetrack-groove O-ring for our main camera hull, and a rectangular gasket for our bottom facing camera. These changes improve camera troubleshooting, and enable camera hot-swapping if necessary.

![CAD model of the front camera hull](/assets/images/seawolfIX/mechanical/camHullExploded.jpg){ .w-[70vw] }

Both our front and bottom camera hulls were designed and manufactured in house, and have passed FEA and real-world waterproofing tests. Our camera hulls attach to SeaWolf's **Fully Modular Frame** via 3D printed mounting brackets, enabling them to be removed for maintenance or for use on a future robot if necessary.
