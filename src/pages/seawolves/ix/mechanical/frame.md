---
title: Fully Modular Frame
sort: 1
---

The SeaWolf IX frame is constructed from 1-inch 6061 aluminum extrusion with #10 holes every half inch. Having a consistent hole pattern allows for convenient mounting of peripherals and sensors at any point along the frame. All extruded frame members have been anodized red via type II anodization to minimize Galvanic corrosion. 

![CAD model of SeaWolf IX's frame](/assets/images/seawolfIX/mechanical/frame.jpg){ .w-[70vw] }

Frame members are held together by a combination of off the shelf and custom 6061 gussets. By standardizing hardware and frame connections, SeaWolf's modular frame is designed to function now while still allowing for additional expansion in the years to come.
