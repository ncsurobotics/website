## Mentorship in FIRST® Robotics
Our members mentor three FTC teams at North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics. In FTC, students create a robot from scratch each year. Teams must compete in alliances with other teams to complete novel technical tasks. Students must additionally document and present season progress, market and communicate their team’s activities, fundraise for team activities, and give back to their respective communities. AquaPack members provide mentorship holistically, providing technical guidance and curating opportunities for experiential learning for 30 youth throughout the September to April build and competition season.


## Bull City Scrimmage  
We also demo SeaWolf VIII and help judge at the FLL pre-season Bull City Scrimmage. In doing this, we contribute to giving approximately 400 students on 33 FTC teams and 24 FLL teams the opportunity to field their robots and practice their interviews in a competition-like environment.
